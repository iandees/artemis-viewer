About

A real-time 3D visualization of NASA's Artemis II mission, the first crewed flight to the Moon in over 50 years.

Orion's position is tracked using live telemetry from NASA's AROW system. The predicted trajectory comes from JPL Horizons ephemeris data. Stars are from the HYG catalog.

Note: Solar panel orientation on the 3D model is approximate and may not reflect the actual spacecraft configuration.
